Part 1: Description of App Features and Services

1.1 Crop Management Dashboard

The App provides farmers the facility to manage their crops digitally. This includes:

  • Active Crops: Users can add their chosen crops to the dashboard, entering crop name, planting date, and land size (in acres).
  • Crop Growth Tracking: Day-by-day progress for each crop is displayed, showing growth stages (Nursery, Vegetative Growth, Flowering Stage, Fruit Development, Harvesting).
  • Task List: An upcoming task list (such as fertiliser application, irrigation, pesticide spraying) is displayed based on the crop lifecycle. Users can mark tasks as complete/incomplete.
  • Financial Ledger: Expense (EXPENSE), Income (INCOME), and net profit/loss are tracked for each crop.
  • Automatic Reminders: Automatic notifications are sent at 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM, reminding farmers of farm-related tasks.

1.2 Digital Mandi (Buy-Sell)

This is a digital marketplace for the buying and selling of agricultural commodities:

  • Posting Advertisements: Users can post their crops or agricultural products as "Available for Sale" or "Wanted to Buy".
  • Location-Based Filter: Users can filter posts by state, district, and tehsil.
  • Contact Facility: Buyers and sellers can contact each other directly via a "Call" button on each post.
  • Reporting System: Any suspicious or inappropriate post can be reported by users. Upon receiving three or more reports, the post is automatically placed in "under_review" status.

1.3 Kisan Charcha (Forum)

This is a community platform where farmers can share experiences, questions, and information:

  • Post Types: Users can post text, photos (JPG), and videos (MP4).
  • Interaction: Like, comment, and share features are available on posts.
  • Location-Based Filter: Users can view posts from their region only.
  • Content Moderation: Inappropriate posts can be reported. Upon receiving three reports, a post is automatically placed under review.

1.4 Nearby Shops

This is a digital directory of local agricultural shops:

  • Shop Registration: Shopkeepers can add their shop details, photo, category, and address.
  • Document Verification: Shopkeepers can upload documents like Aadhaar, Udyam Registration, GST, etc., to get their shop verified.
  • Product Listing: Products (items) can be added under each shop.
  • Premium Membership: Shopkeepers can take a premium membership to make their shop ad-free.

1.5 Advertising Services (VIP Ads / Custom Advertising)

This offers shopkeepers the facility to place targeted advertisements:

  • Ad Creation: Shopkeepers can create an advertisement by selecting a banner, target location (state/district/tehsil), and ad unit.
  • Free Trial: A one-time free trial advertisement is available for new shopkeepers.
  • Paid Advertising: Payment is required for advertisements after the free trial. ₹149 per unit per month (double the fee for 2000+ products).
  • Performance Analytics: The number of views and clicks on the advertisement is tracked.

1.6 Mandi Bhav (Market Prices)

This is a community-driven price information platform:

  • Adding Prices: Users can add market prices for their area.
  • Upvote/Downvote: Other users can upvote or downvote the accuracy of the price.
  • Location-Based Filter: Users can view prices from their selected districts only.

1.7 Kisan Diary

This is a digital diary where farmers can note down daily tasks. Data is backed up to the cloud (Firestore) and is also available offline.

1.8 Live Weather Reporting

Users can report real-time weather for their area (Clear, Sunny, Cloudy, Rain, Storm, Thunderstorm, Fog). Reports are valid for 2 hours and are displayed to the community.

1.9 Government Schemes

The App displays information on government agricultural schemes, including scheme name, department, benefits, deadline, and application link.

1.10 Crop Encyclopedia

Detailed information on various crops is provided — scientific name, climate, soil, varieties, nursery management, fertiliser management, irrigation, weed management, pest and disease control, harvesting and storage.

1.11 Disease Identification

Information on identifying crop diseases and their treatment is provided, including disease name, symptoms, and chemical and organic treatments.

1.12 Fertilizer Calculator

Users can calculate the required quantity of Urea, DAP, and MOP based on their land size and crop.
