Kisan FarmTrack — Privacy Policy

Detailed Privacy Policy of the Kisan FarmTrack mobile application.

1 Introduction and Overview

This Privacy Policy has been prepared by Prime Code Studio — the operator, owner and developer of the Kisan FarmTrack mobile application, whose registered office is located in the State of Chhattisgarh, India. This Policy presents a detailed and transparent account of what information we collect from you, the purposes for which we collect it, and how we use, store, process and secure it, as well as the rights that remain reserved with you in relation to your information.

This Privacy Policy has been drafted in conformity with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and all policies of the Google Play Store currently in force.

The moment you download, install, register on, or in any manner use our App, you shall be deemed to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any provision of this Policy, please immediately stop using the App and uninstall it from your device.

Our App is a comprehensive digital platform for the farmers of India, the farming community, agricultural traders, shopkeepers, tractor owners, seed and fertilizer sellers, and other stakeholders connected with agriculture. The purpose of this App is to bring modern technology to farmers in simple language, to maintain a digital record of their agricultural activities, to provide accurate mandi (market) prices, to assist in the identification of crop diseases, to provide information about government schemes, and to promote mutual cooperation and dialogue among farmers. Since the nature of the App is multi-dimensional, we collect different categories of information for different purposes, and in this Policy we set out a detailed, clear and honest description of each category of information.

2 About Us and How to Contact Us

Our organisation is Prime Code Studio, a software development entity registered in India, and the owner, operator and Data Controller of the Kisan FarmTrack App. The package name of the App is com.farmtrack.kisan and it is distributed on the Google Play Store in India.

All data control, processing and storage relating to this App is the responsibility of Prime Code Studio, and in the event of any dispute, the jurisdiction shall vest in the courts of the State of Chhattisgarh, as clearly stated in the concluding sections of this Policy.

When you begin using our App, you voluntarily, with full mental capacity and without any coercion, consent to all the provisions set out in this Privacy Policy. 4 What Information We Collect

Our App offers a wide range of features, and the operation of each feature requires different types of information. Below we provide a detailed description of each category of information.

4.1 Personal Identification Information

When you register on the App, we collect your full name, ten-digit mobile number, optionally a Gmail address, and the password chosen by you. If you log in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, profile photo and email address from your Google account. This information is stored securely in our Firebase Firestore database and is used to establish the identity of your account, to provide you with a secure login facility, and to link the content created by you to you. Your mobile number serves as the primary identifier for us and is used for OTP verification, account recovery, and for sending essential notifications.

4.2 Location Information

Our App collects information about your location for a number of features. When you fill in the State, District, Tehsil, Block and Village information in your profile, this information is stored on our servers and is used to show you nearby mandi prices, local weather, nearby shops, local advertisements, and farmer discussions from your area. In addition, when you capture a GPS location in the Land Measure feature and in the Tractor Diary, we obtain precise location from your device's GPS sensor in the form of latitude and longitude. This location data is used to measure the boundary of your field, to record the location of tractor work, and to create map links for reaching the customer's field. Location information is collected only when you yourself grant the relevant permission and use the relevant feature.

4.3 Financial and Transaction Information

Our App provides a number of financial tools for farmers, including the Udhaari Khata (Credit Ledger), Interest Calculator, Mandi Calculator, Shop Bill, Global Budget, Tractor Diary, and Currency Calculator. When you use these features, we collect the financial information entered by you, such as the name of the person giving or taking credit, mobile number, village, amount, rate of interest, date, description, payment status; the name of the crop sold in the mandi, quantity, rate, details of deductions; the list of items included in a shop bill, the customer's name and contact details; the details of tractor work, customer information, diesel expenses, wages, and payment status.

All of this information is stored securely in the local SQLite database on your device and, optionally, in your Firebase account under your private collection. The purpose of this information is solely to provide you with the facility of maintaining your financial records, and this information is not shared with any other user or third party. The confidentiality of this information is fully maintained and it is displayed only on the device authorised by you.

4.4 Media and Content Information

When you upload your profile photo, cover photo, videos or photos in Kisan Charcha (the farmer forum), product photos in the Digital Mandi, shop photos, shop product photos, or screenshots in a support ticket, we collect that media content. Before upload, this content is compressed on your device and then stored on a secure cloud storage service called Cloudflare R2. A public link is created for each media file, which is saved in our Firebase Firestore database. When you upload a video, we also generate a thumbnail from it so that it loads quickly in grid view. We use your media content only to operate the relevant feature of the App and make no other commercial use of it.

4.5 Communication and Social Information

When you post in the App's Kisan Charcha (Forum) feature, we collect the message written by you, the caption, the crop name, your name, profile photo, and your location information (State, District, Tehsil). When you follow another farmer, we maintain a list of following and followers. When you block someone, we keep the blocked list secure. When you like, comment on, or share a post, we keep a record of that activity. In addition, we collect the relevant information in order to send you notifications of likes and comments from other farmers. In the support ticket feature, when you record a problem, we keep a record of the description of your problem, the attached screenshots, and all conversations.

4.6 Device and Technical Information

For the operation, security and improvement of the App, we collect certain technical information relating to your device. This includes the manufacturer and model name of your device, the version of the Android operating system, the App version number (Version Code), the device's unique identification number (Android ID), and the Firebase Cloud Messaging token (FCM Token). This technical information helps us maintain the functionality of the App, analyse errors, detect security incidents, and send appropriate notifications to your device. We do not use this information to establish your personal identity; rather, we use it only for overall technical analysis and security.

4.7 App Usage Information

In order to understand which features of our App farmers use the most, we collect certain aggregate usage information. This includes the name of the screen viewed by you, the number of times a particular feature is used, the duration of an App session, and counts of activities such as disease searches and viewing of government schemes. This information is collected in a purely anonymised form and its purpose is only to understand the performance of the App and to add better features in the future. We do not use this information to identify any particular user.

4.8 OTP and Verification Information

When you use an OTP for registration or password recovery, we keep a count of OTP usage based on your mobile number. This includes the number of OTPs sent per day, the number of OTPs sent per month, the total number of OTPs sent over the lifetime, the date on which the last OTP was sent, the number of incorrect OTP attempts, and the blocked status. This information is necessary to prevent misuse of OTPs, to maintain security, and to detect suspicious activity. This information is stored securely, linked to your mobile number and your device's unique identification number.

4.9 Referral Information

When you invite other farmers to use the App, we collect your referral code, the number of invitations sent by you, the number of successful invitations, and information about the reward points earned by you. When a new farmer joins the App through your referral code, we record that new farmer's name, mobile number and time of joining. The purpose of this information is to operate the referral programme, distribute rewards, and measure the effectiveness of the programme.

4.10 Support and Feedback Information

When you contact our support centre, we collect the description of your problem, the screenshots sent by you, and the entire chain of conversation. When you give a rating for the App, we collect the rating given by you, the feedback written by you, and the time of the rating. If you give a four or five star rating, we direct you to rate the App on the Google Play Store, and if you give a lower rating, we seek detailed feedback from you so that we can improve the App.

5 How We Collect Information

We collect your information mainly in three ways.

  1. Information you provide yourself — such as filling in a registration form, updating your profile, adding a credit record, creating a bill, uploading a photo, or posting in the forum.
  2. Information collected automatically through permissions — when you grant the App specific permissions, such as camera permission, location permission or notification permission, the App automatically collects the relevant information.
  3. Information received from Google — when you use Google Sign-In, Google provides us with your name, email and profile photo on the basis of your permission.

In every case, we obtain your explicit consent before collecting information, and you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

We use your information for the following purposes:

  1. To create your account, maintain its security, and provide you with a secure login facility.
  2. To operate all the features of the App — such as maintaining a record of your crops, keeping an account of your financial transactions, measuring your land, keeping a record of your tractor work, and displaying your shop or business online.
  3. To show you accurate mandi prices, local weather, nearby shops, and local farmer discussions based on your location.
  4. To send you important notifications, alerts and updates from time to time.
  5. To detect technical problems in the App, fix errors, and improve the functionality and security of the App.
  6. To resolve your complaints and suggestions and to provide you with better support.
  7. To comply with legal obligations and to cooperate in any legal process.
  8. To understand overall trends in App usage and to add better features in the future.

  5. In the event of a merger, acquisition or reorganisation of our business — in such a case, user information may be transferred to the relevant entity, but even in that situation this Policy shall continue to apply.
